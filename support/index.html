Support

We read every message ourselves.

Found a bug, stuck somewhere, or have an idea for the game? Write to hello@uzigames.com. It helps if you tell us which device you use (for example "iPad, iPadOS 26") and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

Fish: common questions

How do I play? Put your finger anywhere on the screen and slide it: your koi swims that way. Eat fish smaller than you to grow, and stay away from bigger ones. Fish that can eat you have a red outline and red eyes.

I don’t hear any sound. Check the sound button in the top right of the game and your device’s volume. If your iPhone is on silent, try turning silent mode off.

My iPad doesn't vibrate. iPads don't have a vibration motor, so the game can only vibrate on iPhone.

Does it need the internet? No. The game works offline.

I deleted the game and my progress is gone. Progress is saved only on your device (we don't collect any data), so deleting the app deletes it too. Sorry!

Is it safe for kids? Yes: no ads, no purchases, no chat and no data collection. See our privacy policy.
