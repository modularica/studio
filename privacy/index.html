Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Uzi Games is a tiny family studio: Uzay and his dad, Umut, make games together. Our games are made for kids and parents to play together, so we keep things simple and collect as little as possible.

Short version: our games on the App Store (Fish for iPhone and iPad) collect no data at all. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics tools, and the game works with no internet connection.

Our apps (Little Koi for iPhone and iPad)

  • We collect nothing. The game doesn't send anything to us. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Your best length, the fish you've eaten, your koi patterns and pearls are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Game Center. If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best length goes to Game Center so it can show on the leaderboard with your Game Center nickname. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; we don't receive your Apple account details. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings.
  • No ads and no purchases. The game is free. Pearls in the game are not real money and can't be bought.
  • No chat. Players can't send messages to each other.
  • Apple's statistics. If you have agreed in your device settings to share app data with developers, Apple gives us anonymous totals, like the number of downloads and crashes. We never see who you are.

Children

Our games are made to be played by children with their parents. Because our apps collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children either.

Our website and the Lab

The studio page at uzigames.com has no accounts and no tracking. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees the request (like any website that uses Google Fonts).

The Lab (uzigames.com/lab) is a private test area for friends and family, where we try out new games before they are released. It needs a sign-in, and to make it work we store:

  • a username and password (the password is stored securely and we can't read it), and the player names and colors you choose,
  • your game progress and the scores of finished rounds,
  • when and how long you played each game, with a short note like "wave 12",
  • the answers you give when a game asks "How was it?".

Other signed-in Lab players can see player names, scores and who played what. Game saves are private to your account, and "How was it?" answers are read only by us. This data is stored with Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany; the website is hosted by Vercel and the "play with friends" rooms run on Cloudflare. We don't sell or share any of it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

App: delete the app and everything is gone. Lab: email us and we will delete your account and everything connected to it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
